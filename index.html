Stanford CS371

Computational Biology in Four Dimensions

Cutting-edge research frontiers in structural biology, biophysics, and drug discovery. Covers long-timescale allosteric simulations, crowdsourced molecular design (Foldit, EteRNA), coevolutionary deep generative genetics, physics-based alchemical free energy perturbation (FEP+), 3D graph neural networks for docking, continuous Cryo-EM landscapes, and whole-cell spatial dynamics.

Stanford APPHYS 205

Introduction to Biophysics

Physical principles governing the structural, dynamical, and functional organization of biological systems. Covers statistical mechanics of the cell, DNA polymer elasticity (WLC), life at low Reynolds number & diffusion limits, allosteric transitions (MWC), molecular motors & flashing ratchets, Hopfield kinetic proofreading, Hodgkin-Huxley electrophysiology, and bacterial chemotaxis.
